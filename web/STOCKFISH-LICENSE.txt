Stockfish.js — Stockfish compiled to WebAssembly
================================================

Files:
  stockfish-18-lite-single.js
  stockfish-18-lite-single.wasm

These are unmodified build artifacts of Stockfish.js 18, redistributed here so
the web build can analyse positions locally.
